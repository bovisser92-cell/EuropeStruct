Our Services

EuropeStruct is active across four disciplines: New Construction, Utility Construction, Renovation & Remodeling and Construction Management & Advisory. Read on to see what each of these pillars covers.

01 — New Construction

Building from the ground up

EuropeStruct delivers new-build projects for private and commercial clients: from residential homes to commercial buildings. We guide the project from the first sketch through to turnkey completion.

Every new-build project gets a tailored approach, matched to location, programme of requirements and budget — with short lines between design and execution.

  • Residential construction — detached, semi-detached and project-based
  • New commercial buildings
  • Foundation work and shell construction
  • Finishing and turnkey delivery
02 — Utility Construction

Functional buildings for every purpose

Utility construction covers the delivery of industrial buildings, offices, schools and healthcare or sports facilities. EuropeStruct works closely with clients, architects and installers to make these buildings functional and future-proof.

From a logistics warehouse to an educational or healthcare building: we translate the programme of requirements into a building that works for its users.

  • Industrial and logistics buildings
  • Office buildings
  • Schools, healthcare and sports facilities
  • Delivery from foundation to completion
03 — Renovation & Remodeling

New life for existing property

EuropeStruct renovates, remodels and upgrades the sustainability of existing homes and commercial buildings. We carefully assess the existing structure and match the approach to today's requirements.

Whether it's an extension, an internal remodel or an energy-efficiency renovation: we make sure old and new work connect seamlessly.

  • Renovation of homes and commercial buildings
  • Extensions and additions
  • Sustainability upgrades and energy-efficiency renovation
  • Internal remodeling and layout changes
04 — Construction Management & Advisory

Oversight and direction for your project

EuropeStruct provides construction management in the broadest sense: site supervision, planning, quality and cost control. We act as the directing party within your construction project.

Good construction management is the backbone of every successful project. It prevents delays, miscommunication and cost overruns. We monitor progress so you can focus on your own business.

  • Site supervision and project coordination
  • Planning and direction of contractors
  • Monitoring scope, budget and schedule
  • Reporting and quality control
